Our Process

Simple, transparent, professional.

We believe in keeping things simple and stress-free for our clients. From your first call to the final walkthrough, here's exactly how we work.

1
Free Quote

Call or message us and we'll come to you. We assess the job in person, discuss your needs and colour choices, and provide a detailed written quote with no obligation whatsoever.

2
Book Your Date

Accept the quote and choose a start date that works for your schedule. We confirm in writing and arrive on time, every time — no last-minute cancellations or no-shows.

3
We Paint

Our team arrives fully equipped and ready to work. We protect your property, complete thorough surface preparation, then apply premium coatings with professional skill and care.

4
You Love It

We complete a detailed final walkthrough with you to ensure everything meets your expectations. We clean up fully and don't consider the job done until you're completely happy.
