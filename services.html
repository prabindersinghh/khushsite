Why Choose a Local Brisbane Painter?

Choosing a locally based painting company like Paintmates means you get a team that truly understands Brisbane's unique climate conditions — the intense summer heat, UV exposure, humidity and tropical storms that can take a toll on paint finishes faster than in cooler climates.

We recommend the right products for Queensland conditions, apply appropriate primers and top coats for maximum durability, and take the time to properly prepare surfaces so our finishes look great and last for years.
